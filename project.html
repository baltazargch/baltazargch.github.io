Applied · 2024 · Rift Valley, Kenya

Groundwater access mapping, Rift Valley

Mapped seasonal water access gaps across 40 rural communities so drilling budgets could be aimed at the villages that run dry first. The county water office now uses the layer in its annual planning cycle.

The problem

Two or three sentences on what was wrong or unknown before this work. Keep it concrete — a specific gap, not a general lament about data scarcity.

Approach

  • Sentinel-2 NDWI time series, 2019–2024, monthly composites
  • Field verification at 40 sites across two dry seasons
  • Household survey data joined at ward level in PostGIS
